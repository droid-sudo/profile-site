Motivation and boundaries

Why I am donating

First of all, I would love to have biological descendants and leave a part of me in the world. More importantly, I am also very happy to help others build their families, as I understand how important and sometimes difficult this process can be. I believe everyone deserves the opportunity to experience the joy of having a family, regardless of their background or circumstances. I am also proud to support LGBTQ+ communities and minority groups. Being able to help someone fulfill their dream of becoming a parent would mean a lot to me. To me, this is more meaningful and fulfilling than getting into an Ivy League school, publishing countless papers, or making millions of dollars working in Big Tech.

What I look for in intended parents

  • Ready and committed to raising a child
  • Emotionally and financially stable
  • Preferably in a stable relationship with your partner
  • Willing to tell the child about donor conception in an age-appropriate way
