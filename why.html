Motivation and boundaries

Why I am donating

I understand that building a family can be difficult. I am willing to help intended parents through a responsible known-donor arrangement if we are a good mutual fit.

I do not request compensation for the donation or charge for the sperm vials. Recipients are responsible for their own legal, clinic, testing, and shipping costs unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Role and boundaries

Role
Donor only, not a parent
Custody
No custody or decision-making role intended
Agreement
Independent attorneys and a signed known-donor agreement
Adult contact
Open to child-initiated contact at age 18, under agreed terms

What I look for in intended parents

  • Ready and committed to raising a child
  • Comfortable with legal and medical review
  • Clear about the donor’s role and future contact
  • Willing to tell the child about donor conception in an age-appropriate way
