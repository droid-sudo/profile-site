Beyond the quick facts

A curious mind, an active life, and strong family roots.

I hope this page gives you a more human sense of who I am—what shaped me, what I care about, and how I move through the world.

Where I come from

Raised with support and room to explore

I grew up close to an extended family, surrounded by friends and cousins. My family was warm, supportive, and encouraging of new experiences. My mother’s care and my father’s thoughtful perspective both shaped me; together, they gave me curiosity, a love of learning, and confidence to follow ideas seriously.

That background is part of why I approach donation with care. Family is not an abstract idea to me—it is the everyday work of showing up, listening, and giving someone space to become themselves.

Education & research

I’ve always been drawn to difficult questions.

  1. Early academic interests

    Mathematics and physics first taught me the pleasure of finding structure inside something complicated. I earned a top 1% score on the college entrance exam and received awards in math and physics competitions.

  2. Undergraduate engineering

    I earned a bachelor’s degree in Telecommunications Engineering, building foundations in mathematics, programming, signal processing, and control. I also participated in mathematical modeling and electrical design competitions.

  3. Graduate study & research

    I am now pursuing graduate study and research in electrical and computer engineering. Research suits my temperament: patient, curious, and willing to keep working when an answer is not obvious.

Life outside work

Movement keeps me balanced.

Basketball has been a long-term part of my life, including school teams in high school and college. I also enjoy swimming, gym training, running, tennis, and trying new outdoor activities. Staying active gives me energy and keeps academic life in perspective.

Beyond sports, I enjoy history, applied mathematics, learning how systems work, good conversations, and time with people close to me.
